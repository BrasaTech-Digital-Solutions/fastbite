5. **The Working Meeting:** Meet with the Concejalía and AFIC technical staff to review the document and agree on logistical cooperation (see Section 2).

   - Contact the Cámara de Comercio's *Oficina Acelera Pyme* (`acelerapyme@camaralicante.com`) and local hospitality/merchant associations **only after** receiving the town hall's initial response.
   - Ask the AFIC team during the meeting to introduce you or validate the active contact details for local association boards.

## 4. Project Risks & Strategic Mitigation

Be prepared to navigate these practical constraints:

1. **Municipal Neutrality Mandate (Ley 9/2017 & Ley 38/2003):**
   - *Risk:* The Ayuntamiento cannot endorse, procure, or mandate FastBite to businesses without a public tender.
   - *Mitigation:* Explicitly emphasize that you are asking for informational diffusion and room access, not public funding or exclusivity. Accept that their communication will frame this as an open pilot open to local innovation.
2. **AEAT Production Environment Timing:**
   - *Risk:* FastBite is currently validated against the pre-production testbed (`prewww1.aeat.es`). If restaurants expect live tax submission on day one, misunderstandings can arise.
   - *Mitigation:* Pitch the pilot primarily on operational efficiency (QR ordering, kitchen display, allergen safety), say that FastBite already creates the chained records and the QR on every ticket, and that sending to the AEAT will be switched on before each restaurant's legal deadline; give no date until it is live.
3. **Onboarding & Support Bottlenecks in High Season:**
   - *Risk:* Onboarding 10–15 hospitality venues simultaneously during peak seasonal operations can strain support capacity.
   - *Mitigation:* Stagger pilot entries in cohorts of 3–5 venues. Prioritize tech-savvy local managers for the initial cohort.
4. **Data Protection Formalities (RGPD):**
   - *Risk:* Resistance to administrative paperwork regarding guest data.
   - *Mitigation:* Clarify that the standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is integrated into the online registration flow (`/legal/data-processing`), requiring no separate paper bureaucracy.
5. **Regulatory Delay Risks (VERI*FACTU Deadlines):**
   - *Risk:* If the Spanish government postpones the 2027 deadlines again, the perceived regulatory urgency drops.
   - *Mitigation:* Never sell FastBite solely on the basis of avoiding fines. Lead with turnover speed, reduced order errors, digital allergen compliance, and modern customer experience.
