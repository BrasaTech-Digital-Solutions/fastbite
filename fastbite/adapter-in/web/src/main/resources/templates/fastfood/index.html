A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before you
open the doors.

What happens after the free trial?

Your trial lasts 30 days from registration. You won’t be charged automatically. Subscribe to continue taking new orders. Otherwise, you can still manage existing orders and export your data.

Do I need a card to get started?

No. Try the full workflow first. Activating a paid subscription before the trial ends starts billing immediately; checkout shows what you’ll pay.

Are payment fees included?

Payment-processing fees and applicable taxes are separate. FastBite does not add its own per-order commission.

Can I use my existing tablet or computer?

FastBite runs in a web browser on a connected device. Test your devices during the trial. Dedicated receipt printers, cash drawers and offline operation are not included.

Can I add more locations?

Yes. Each has its own menu, team, trial and subscription, managed from one owner console. For three or more locations, request a volume quote before subscribing. Billing is currently per location.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Data export is included, even after your trial or subscription ends. Download your restaurant backup from the owner console.
